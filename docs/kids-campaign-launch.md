# Kids campaign launch

## Website

- `/kids`: family hub, linked from desktop/mobile navigation, homepage, footer and party pages.
- `/chicago/kids-birthday-parties` and `/eugene/kids-birthday-parties`: city-specific birthday inquiries.
- `/chicago/kids-pottery-classes` and `/eugene/kids-pottery-classes`: proposed schedule and parent opt-in.
- Birthday inquiries use the existing `private-party` Netlify form with occasion `Kids birthday party`. Class interest uses `kids-class-interest`. Review both in Netlify Forms. The new interest form collects permission; it does not automatically send marketing emails or create bookings.
- Parents provide their own contact information. No child's full name, birthday or school is requested. Analytics receive categorical events, not form text.
- Each city page has its own canonical, title, description, visible FAQ and breadcrumb data. All five routes are in the sitemap.

## Launch sequence

1. Publish the family pages and inquiry forms; check daily for parent inquiries.
2. Add kids birthday services and city landing links to existing Google, Yelp and Nextdoor profiles. Avoid creating duplicate studio profiles.
3. Submit local parent-directory listings after confirming price and terms. Prioritize ChicagoKids, Chicago Parent, Eugene Kids Guide and Oregon Family.
4. Once a real public session has a confirmed date, instructor, capacity and booking link, submit eligible event calendars. Private birthday availability is not a public event.
5. Review weekly: birthday inquiries, qualified inquiries, quotes, paid reservations, class interest by city, directory source and inquiry-to-booking rate.
